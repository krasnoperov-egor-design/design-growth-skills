# Professional Growth

This module contains four AI-guided skills for professional reflection and development.

| Skill | Main question |
| --- | --- |
| [Current Professional Profile](current-professional-profile/SKILL.md) | How do I actually operate as a professional today? |
| [Annual Reflection](annual-reflection/SKILL.md) | How did I change over a meaningful period? |
| [PDP Builder](pdp-builder/SKILL.md) | What should I deliberately develop next? |
| [PDP Progress Check](pdp-progress-check/SKILL.md) | Is my development plan actually working? |

The skills can be used independently or combined into a longer development cycle. For example, a Current Professional Profile or Annual Reflection can inform PDP Builder; a PDP can later be revisited with PDP Progress Check. Share any earlier output you want the next skill to use.

Open the relevant `SKILL.md` file and give it to your AI assistant, or paste its contents into a conversation. The assistant can guide the discussion in your language. These reflections support your judgment alongside real work, feedback, and any career framework relevant to you.

Looking for feedback on a portfolio or resume? See [Career and Job Search](../career-job-search/README.md).
